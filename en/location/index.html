About Einruhr

Einruhr is an idyllic village in the Eifel National Park, located right on the shore of the Obersee (part of Lake Rursee). The village offers the perfect mix of nature, tranquility, and relaxation, combined with numerous opportunities for activities in the area.

With its location on the water and at the edge of the national park, Einruhr is the ideal starting point for hiking, cycling, and water sports. You'll also find cozy restaurants and cafés where you can enjoy local specialties.

Eifel National Park

The Eifel National Park, founded in 2004, covers an area of about 110 square kilometers and is home to unique wildlife and plant life. Here you can explore untouched nature on numerous hiking trails.

Particularly impressive are the extensive beech forests, the clear streams, and the diverse wildlife of the park. With a bit of luck, you can spot wildcats, black woodpeckers, or even the rare black stork.

Gastronomy

In and around Einruhr you'll find numerous cozy restaurants and cafés offering local specialties and international cuisine. Enjoy the regional cuisine with fresh products from the Eifel.

Particularly popular are traditional Eifel dishes such as "Himmel un Ääd" (Heaven and Earth), game specialties, and the delicious apple cake, which has a long tradition here.
