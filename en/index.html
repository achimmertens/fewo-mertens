Comfortable Holiday Apartment

Our lovingly furnished holiday apartment in Einruhr offers space for up to 4 people and is equipped with everything you need for a relaxing vacation.

Ideal Location

The holiday apartment is located in the idyllic village of Einruhr, right next to the Eifel National Park and the picturesque Obersee lake – just a few minutes from Lake Rursee.
