Activities in the Area

Discover exciting destinations and activities around Einruhr in the Eifel National Park.

Guided Hiking Tours

Right next to our house happens to live the chairman of the Eifelverein Einruhr-Erkensruhr, Frank Wiesen. He regularly offers guided hiking tours through the Eifel National Park, suitable for both beginners and experienced hikers. The tours lead through the beautiful landscapes of the immediate surroundings and offer the opportunity to discover the diverse flora and fauna of the region. Please check the Eifelverein Einruhr-Erkensruhr website for dates.

Eifelverein Einruhr-Erkensruhr

Bread Baking in Erkensruhr

In the small half-timbered bakehouse "Et Backes", fresh stone-oven bread is baked every first Saturday of the month. Wheat-rye mixed bread on sourdough basis — straight from the oven. Sales from approx. 10:30 AM. The bakehouse, in the neighboring village of Erkensruhr about 3 km away, is run by the Bürgerverein Erkensruhr/Hirschrott e.V. A real experience for anyone who appreciates traditional craftsmanship!

Et Backes – Bakehouse Erkensruhr

The Red House in Monschau

The Red House Museum in Monschau offers a fascinating journey back to the 18th century. The cloth manufacturer Johann Heinrich Scheibler had this representative residence built around 1760. With 13 fully furnished living rooms, precious canvas wallpapers, and the world-famous grand staircase made of oak, it is an unforgettable experience. Open from April to November, Tuesday to Sunday.

Red House Museum Monschau

Guided Ranger Tours in the Eifel National Park

Experience the Eifel National Park up close on a guided ranger tour. The certified rangers of the national park take you on exciting discovery tours through beech forests, across wild meadows, and along picturesque waterways. You'll learn interesting facts about the local flora and fauna as well as the special features of the protected area. Tours take place regularly — current dates and registration on the National Park website.

Canoe & Bicycle Rental in Rurberg

Rent canoes or bicycles in Rurberg, just a few kilometers from Einruhr. Explore the picturesque landscape of the Eifel National Park on the water or on two wheels.
