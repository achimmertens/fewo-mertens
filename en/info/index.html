Frequently Asked Questions

Are pets allowed?

Yes, pets are allowed in our holiday apartment outside the bedroom. However, we ask that you remove all traces of pets (hair, dirt) before departure.

Is there parking?

Yes, there is free parking directly at the house.

Is WiFi available?

No, we do not offer WiFi. However, mobile reception is very good.

Where to put the trash?

There are waste bins for residual waste, recyclables, and paper in front of the house. Bottles should be taken with you or disposed of in the residual waste.

Do we have to pay for electricity and heating?

No. We assume that you will use resources responsibly. You are welcome to use the firewood provided in baskets for the fireplace. We wouldn't mind a small donation ;-). Please keep in mind that the apartment will be cold when you first arrive. The fireplace should normally be prepared. The shower water is warm during high season. (In winter we turn it off, but can turn it back on).

How does the key handover work?

After you have sent us a reservation request and we have agreed on the conditions, you will receive our bank details. After payment is received, you will receive the description of the key safe and the corresponding code. Please put the key back in the same safe when you leave.

How can I book?

You can inquire directly via our Price Calculator or reach us by phone at +49 1517 4412216 or 02403 837412.
