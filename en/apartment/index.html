The Apartment

Discover our cozy and fully equipped holiday apartment in the heart of the Eifel National Park.

Your Home in Einruhr

Our lovingly furnished holiday apartment is located in the beautiful village of Einruhr, right on the Obersee lake and at the edge of the Eifel National Park.

The apartment comfortably accommodates up to 4 people, with a living room, a bedroom, a bathroom, a kitchenette, and an outdoor terrace. The furnishings are completely new (2023), stylishly chosen and offer you all the comfort for a relaxing stay.

The Living Room

The living room features a pull-out sofa bed that comfortably sleeps two additional people.

The soft seating area faces the fireplace, which provides both cozy warmth and a sense of calm. Here you can enjoy the beautiful view of the forest, relax in the evening, or read a book.

Heating & Fireplace

The apartment does not have conventional heating, but instead features a beautiful, cozy fireplace that provides pleasant warmth and a special atmosphere on cool days. There is plenty of free firewood available. We would appreciate a small donation though. The fireplace is easy to operate. If you need instructions, you can find them in our tutorial video.

The Bedroom

The apartment features a high-quality box spring bed (180x200 cm)

Bathroom

The bathroom is equipped with a shower. Towels can be booked as a linen package upon request.

The Kitchen

The open kitchenette is fully equipped, so you can cater for yourself during your stay. You'll find everything you need for cooking: stove, refrigerator with freezer, French press for coffee, kettle, toaster, as well as dishes and cooking utensils. We also always have some food supplies in the cupboard for you, which you may take for a small donation.

Terrace

The apartment has a beautiful terrace where you can relax and enjoy nature in good weather. The Eifel National Park begins right next to and behind the terrace — you're practically sitting in the middle of it and can sometimes spot rare bird species, dormice, or other animals.
